# Measurement

A duration measures elapsed time between two instants. This is an explanatory
definition, rather than a claim about the current state of a machine.

An observation needs a date or instant to say when it was made. A date such as
`2026-10-16` has calendar precision. An instant such as `2026-10-16T12:00:00Z`
identifies a point on the UTC timeline. They should not be silently interchanged.

Recorded time describes the knowledge system's history. Valid time describes the
assertion's subject. A correction made tomorrow can concern yesterday's measurement.
