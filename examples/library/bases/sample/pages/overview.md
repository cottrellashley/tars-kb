# Synthetic sample knowledge base

This small base contains fictional teaching material. It has no personal records,
machine inventory, credentials or live service claims.

Read [Measurement](measurement.md) for a durable definition and
[Demonstration garden](garden.md) for examples that need observation and validity dates.

The examples distinguish when an assertion was recorded from when its subject was
observed or its arrangement is valid. A source citation is evidence metadata; the
CLI never fetches its URI as a side effect of reading this base.
