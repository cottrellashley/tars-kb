# Demonstration garden

This garden is fictional. Its plot count illustrates an observed assertion that may
later be corrected or replaced by a newly dated observation.

A temporary watering trial illustrates a finite validity interval. An interval can
end while the record remains available as historical evidence. Expiration does not
erase the assertion or prove what arrangement replaced it.

Use structured assertions for changing values and their dates. Keep this page for
the explanation, avoiding a second competing source of the current plot count.
